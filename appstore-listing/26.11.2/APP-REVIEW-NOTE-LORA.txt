New in 26.11.2: LoRa (optional)

psst 26.11.2 adds an optional LoRa section. It lets psst send and receive short text messages over a Meshtastic
LoRa radio that the user owns, connected to the iPhone over Bluetooth Low Energy. This is useful where there is
no mobile network or internet. Direct LoRa messages between two psst users who have linked their keys are
additionally end-to-end encrypted by psst (X25519 + ChaCha20-Poly1305); LoRa messages go from radio to radio
and do not pass through psst servers.

- The feature is optional and needs a Meshtastic LoRa radio (third-party hardware). Without a radio the app is
  fully functional: chats, groups, calls, files and location sharing do not depend on LoRa.
- Bluetooth permission is requested only when the user opens LoRa and taps "Search for devices via Bluetooth…".
  psst does not use Bluetooth anywhere else, does not scan in the background and does not use Bluetooth to
  determine location.

How to see the screen without a radio:
1. Sign in with the review account (see the notes above).
2. On the home screen (chat list), tap the antenna button in the top bar (accessibility label "LoRa").
   The LoRa screen opens.
3. Tap "Search for devices via Bluetooth…". iOS shows the Bluetooth permission prompt with our purpose text
   ("psst uses Bluetooth only when you open LoRa, to connect to your own Meshtastic LoRa radio and send and
   receive LoRa messages. It does not track other devices or your location.").
4. Without a radio nearby the list stays empty ("Searching for Meshtastic radios…"); nothing else is needed.
   The rest of the app works the same whether Bluetooth is allowed or denied.
