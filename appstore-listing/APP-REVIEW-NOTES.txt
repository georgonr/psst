psst is an invitation-only messenger: new accounts can only be created with an invitation code from
the operator, so please use this ready-made test account. No sign-up is needed.

1. Open psst and tap "Continue" / "Sign in". The sign-in page of our server (chat.psst.pro) opens.
2. Username: review   Password: <PASSWORD — filled in by the owner, see ~/proj/psst-review.txt>
3. psst then asks you to confirm your identity on this new device. Choose "Use recovery key"
   (Android: "Enter recovery key") and enter: <RECOVERY KEY — filled in by the owner>
   This unlocks the encrypted history. (Do not choose "Reset identity", or the old messages
   become unreadable.)
4. You will see a direct chat with "Review Partner" and a group "Weekend trip". You can send
   messages, photos and voice messages there.

Reporting content: long-press (iPhone: touch and hold) a message → Report → enter a reason → Send.
A whole room: open the chat → tap its name → Report room. Blocking a user: tap their name or
avatar → Block. Reports go to the operator's admin page and are reviewed within 24 hours.

Deleting the account: Settings (tap your avatar) → Manage account & devices → the account page opens → Delete account → confirm. The same steps and an e-mail option
are described at https://psst.pro/delete-account. If you delete the review account, e-mail
psst@psst.pro and we will provide a new one.
