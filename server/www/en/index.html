What psst does

Everything you expect from a messenger, without ads and without someone else's cloud holding your messages.

  • Encrypted messages

    A message is encrypted on your device. Only you and the person you write to can read it.

  • Voice and video calls

    Call one person or a whole group. Calls are encrypted between devices too.

  • Groups

    Family, friends, colleagues – each with their own conversation, with replies, reactions and mentions.

  • Files and photos

    Send a photo, video, document or voice message. Encrypted, just like text.

  • Our own server

    Messages and files are stored on our own server, not in another company's cloud.

  • Invitation only

    Only people invited by the administrator can create an account. Strangers can't sign up on their own.

What the server can and can't see

It can't read

  • the content of messages and files
  • the content of voice and video calls
  • photos, videos and voice messages

It can see, like any messenger

  • who talks to whom, and when
  • group names and members
  • that your device connects – the network you use can see that too

Phone notifications are delivered by Google or Apple – without message content.

Where psst runs

On your phone and your computer; conversations stay in sync across devices.

  • Android
  • Windows
  • macOS
  • Web browser
  • iPhone coming soon

Access is by invitation only

Invitations come from the psst administrator. If you have one, download the app and enter the invitation code when you create your account.
